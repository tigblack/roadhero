Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a finished 3D-stylized enemy bestiary CONCEPT SHEET for REGION 3 of SCARBOUND, a mobile isometric idle arcade car-combat RPG. Region identity is ASHEN WINTER, the ash-covered irradiated wasteland in a long nuclear winter. Exactly SEVEN distinct full-body designs, all on one clean professional presentation board, ready to guide Blender modelers. Five regular creatures in the upper row; the combat snow vehicle lower left; the massive boss lower right taking the largest area. Leave clear gaps so every silhouette and limb is visible. Give every design a small isolated ash-and-snow ground contact and soft cast shadow, on a neutral very light cool-gray backdrop. Render from consistent elevated three-quarter views, enough above to show what reads from the game camera. No cropped limbs, no overlapping designs.

Shared visual language: stylized three-dimensional sculpted game characters, chunky readable forms, broad bevels, expressive shapes, deliberate anatomy, tactile matte materials with controlled roughness. Menacing and uncanny, without gore, and never adorable. Characters are charcoal-black and dark graphite with dirty ivory frost, muted slate blue-gray in shadow, corroded steel and small desaturated orange hazard details. Only a few tiny hot amber focal points. Bodies must remain darker than the pale snow environment. Dirty frost and ash are physical deposits with broad readable shapes, not a blinding bloom or huge layer of blue crystals. This is industrial nuclear aftermath, not fantasy ice magic.

Seven numbered designs with the following exact silhouettes and identities:
01 ASH PECKER. A small vicious TWO-LEGGED mutated scavenger bird, hunched curved neck, long hollow metal-edged beak like a short tube that spits jagged scrap. Small useless wings, ragged burnt feather masses and a few frost clumps. Thin quick legs beneath a compact teardrop body; nothing dog-like or frog-like. Readable ranged scavenger silhouette, not a human wearing a bird mask.
02 SNOW REAPER. Fast crouched predatory mutant, small sunken head between high shoulders, two enormous forward hooked ice-pick forelimbs framing a sharp downward chevron, powerful short hind legs and long trailing spine. Black charred hide and dull bone-colored rime plates, neither a crab nor a mantis and no external carried weapon. Hooks are anatomical blades; this is the dangerous close-combat attacker.
03 NEEDLE WALKER. A tall slender living TRIPOD supported by exactly THREE long articulated rebar-like legs. An asymmetrical charred torso tangled permanently with a bundle of steel javelins in a horizontal launcher across its upper body. Small recessed amber sensor/eye. Its three-legged stance is very clear, all legs visible. An organic-industrial mutant that walks, not a static turret. It fires long physical needles, not magic.
04 BOILER SPITTER. A LOW WIDE potbellied mutant fused into a broken insulated boiler, four short splayed supports, a huge slanted chimney-like organic-industrial mortar opening on its back. Ribbed bellows and broken ceramic insulation at joints; its furnace opening has a restrained amber coal glow. Clearly a squat oval artillery enemy, with a visible wide projectile mouth, not a frog, toad or gas-masked human.
05 CONCRETE EXECUTIONER. A HEAVY RECTANGULAR bipedal mutant/exoskeleton, tiny deeply recessed head, broad cracked concrete-and-lead shoulder slabs, enormous industrial shotgun arm with a clear square muzzle integrated into the limb, a shorter bracing arm and thick short legs. The silhouette is a broad block, not a long-legged walker. Frost clings to slab edges, exposed dark sinew joins the structure. Not an ordinary person in a colored winter coat. This enemy delivers a heavy short-range ranged blast.
06 WHITEOUT RAIDER. A long LOW ARMORED COMBAT SNOWMOBILE, two widely spaced steerable front SKIS and a clearly visible central rear rubber TRACK, sharp wedge snowplow nose, open compact rotating machine-gun turret, riveted graphite body, dirty white frost streaks, a tiny hooded driver largely hidden in the armored cockpit. Mechanical, gritty, buildable vehicle proportions. Visibly a dedicated snow vehicle, never the swamp buggy with a green eye pasted on it.
07 BLACK REACTOR, the BOSS. A towering NINE-METRE BIPEDAL mutant made of a ruined nuclear reactor containment body: TWO massive walking legs, reactor-vessel torso with a small exposed hot amber core in a dark circular cavity, heavy cracked concrete shielding around the shoulders, one huge asymmetrical crushing arm and a smaller bracing/claw arm, broken dark exhaust stacks and hazard-striped metal around the back. Small head or sensory slit buried under the upper shielding; do not give it a human face. Huge hunched vertical silhouette, frost-covered shielding and charcoal ash deposits. Its main read is a walking reactor, not a truck, tree creature, four-legged swamp mother or a blue ice golem. Boss must be much more massive and taller in implied scale than all regular enemies and dominate the lower-right composition.

Use only large clean numerals 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07 next to the corresponding designs. A small title ASHEN WINTER at top is allowed. No other written names, no descriptive paragraphs, no fake game UI, no logo or watermark. Design complete bodies, all seven precisely once. Avoid green acid, purple swamp flesh, mushrooms, branches, root legs, lush moss, Christmas snow, cute plush monsters, high-frequency realistic gore and shiny toy plastic. The result should be cohesive with a casual stylized 3D postapocalyptic car-combat game, with seven strongly different silhouettes clear even in grayscale. Wide landscape, crisp high-resolution presentation.
