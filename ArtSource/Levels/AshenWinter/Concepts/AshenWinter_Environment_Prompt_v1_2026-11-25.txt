Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished landscape environment concept art board for REGION 3 of SCARBOUND, a mobile isometric idle arcade car-combat RPG. The proposed region is ASHEN WINTER: the wasteland years AFTER nuclear fallout, in nuclear winter. The visual must be suitable as a direct Blender modeling reference for an existing colorful stylized 3D game.

Art direction: visibly stylized three-dimensional mobile game render, chunky sculpted geometry, large simple planes, softened bevels, carefully controlled detail, attractive readable silhouettes and broad matte material changes. Slightly sinister but accessible. Not photorealistic, not a grim realistic AAA war photograph, not an anime illustration, not a vector drawing, not pixel art. Keep shapes buildable as optimized 3D meshes.

Composition: a large hero view occupies the upper three quarters of the image, from a high isometric game-camera angle. The lower strip contains THREE clearly separated detail vignettes of the same region: 1) a highway ramp and its safe landing area, 2) a snow-buried bunker tunnel with an obviously usable vehicle entrance and exit, 3) a reactor boss courtyard. These are coherent details of one environment, not unrelated biomes. A small graphite armored muscle-car pickup on the main road provides scale; the world is several times more prominent than the car. No HUD, no interface, no captions or labels.

Hero environment: a clear dark asphalt highway snakes through dirty gray-white snowdrifts mixed with black nuclear ash. Strong black char bands and patches of ash interrupt the snow, with a visible road surface. A burnt dead forest of broad broken black trunks, low irregular ash dunes, collapsed concrete overpasses, wrecked abandoned vehicles half-buried in drifts, rusted electrical pylons, bunker air intakes, thick district-heating pipes and a ruined industrial district make this clearly a nuclear aftermath rather than a generic ski landscape. Far behind is a recognizable ruined nuclear power station with a cracked cooling tower and collapsed reactor building, silhouetted cleanly against an overcast pale sky. Tiny amber emergency lamps are the only warm lights. Fine falling ash and a little drifting powder sell the wind without obscuring the play area.

Gameplay layout: make routes clearly driveable, a broad winding road with two alternative shortcuts, a concrete jump with sufficient run-up and broad snow-cleared landing, a raised viaduct, a low traversable valley and a bunker passage. Use industrial landmarks to guide the route to a circular boss arena beside the reactor. The reactor arena is an open wide concrete containment basin dusted with ash and snow, with a circular broken shielding ring, road access, some low cover, and a small intense amber reactor opening in the far background. Several large dark enemy silhouettes may be distant atmosphere only, never the main subject; the enemy roster will be on a separate sheet.

Palette roles: dirty bone-white snow and pale cement; charcoal-black ash, burnt timber and wrecks; slate blue-gray shadows and frosted steel; desaturated rusty red-orange in tiny industrial accents; restrained hot amber in emergency lamps and the reactor core. Road is a darker midgray so its borders are legible. No green acid, no swamp, no mushrooms, no roots, no wet tropical vegetation. Avoid bright turquoise fantasy ice crystals or magical ice castles. Ice is dull gray rime on broken concrete and metal. Cold diffuse daylight still lets all objects read at phone scale.

The nuclear winter should look beautiful, dangerous and fun to drive through, with strong contrast between the ashen snow and dark silhouettes, not like a flat empty white map. No active mushroom-cloud explosion, no ongoing air raid. No logos, watermark, UI, decorative typography or written captions. Crisp high-resolution wide landscape art board.
